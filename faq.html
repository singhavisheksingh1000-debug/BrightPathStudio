Frequently Asked Questions

Are BrightPathStudio products digital?

Yes. Products are digital downloads unless a product description says otherwise. There is no physical shipping.

How do I receive my purchase?

Purchases are delivered through the checkout provider used on the product page. Follow the download instructions shown after purchase.

Can I print the products?

Printable products are designed for home or professional printing. Check each product description for its intended format.

Can I use a digital planner on a tablet?

Digital planners may be designed for compatible note-taking apps and devices. Check the individual product description before purchasing.
