50 Indoor Activities for Kids

When outdoor plans are not possible, a simple activity list can save the day. Mix creative, educational, active and quiet ideas.

Creative ideas

Try drawing challenges, paper crafts, paper crowns, sticker scenes, homemade bookmarks, cardboard builds, collage art, sock puppets, paper plates and recycled-material sculptures.

Puzzles and learning

Use word searches, mazes, matching games, scavenger hunts, simple math games, spot-the-difference pages, riddles and trivia.

Movement and pretend play

Create an indoor obstacle course, dance challenge, balloon volleyball, animal walks, freeze dance, pretend restaurant, mini theater or treasure hunt.

Quiet-time activities

Reading corners, journaling, coloring, origami, memory games, story writing and simple breathing activities can help children transition to calmer time.
