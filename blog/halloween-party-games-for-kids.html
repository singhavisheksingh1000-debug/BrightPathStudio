25 Halloween Party Games for Kids 🎃 Easy & Fun Ideas

Planning a Halloween party for kids? Skip the last-minute scramble. Here are 25 easy Halloween party games and activities you can use at home, at a classroom celebration, at a Halloween sleepover, or at a family gathering.

From Halloween bingo and scavenger hunts to charades, coloring and creative challenges, these ideas are designed to be simple to prepare and easy to adapt for different ages. Many are also screen-free Halloween activities, so kids can spend more of the celebration creating, moving and playing together.

25 Halloween party games for kids

  1. Halloween Bingo: Use picture cards featuring pumpkins, ghosts, bats, candy, witches and friendly monsters. Great for younger children and classroom Halloween parties.
  2. Halloween Scavenger Hunt: Give kids a checklist of things to find, such as something orange, a pumpkin, a bat, a spider or something spooky.
  3. Halloween I Spy: Choose a room and challenge children to find Halloween colors, shapes and decorations.
  4. Pin the Spider on the Web: Put a large paper web on the wall and let players place a paper spider as close to the center as possible.
  5. Halloween Charades: Act out ghost, witch, vampire, skeleton, zombie, bat, pumpkin or monster without saying the word.
  6. Halloween Freeze Dance: Play Halloween music and freeze whenever the music stops. Add silly monster poses for extra laughs.
  7. Halloween Would You Rather: Ask funny questions such as whether players would rather be a ghost or a vampire.
  8. Pumpkin Drawing Challenge: Give everyone a pumpkin outline and award playful categories such as funniest, cutest or most creative.
  9. Halloween Memory Game: Create pairs of Halloween pictures and place them face down for a simple matching game.
  10. Halloween Word Search: Use words such as pumpkin, ghost, bat, witch, candy, spider and monster.
  11. Halloween Mystery Box: Put safe, familiar objects inside covered boxes or bags and let children guess what they are by touch.
  12. Halloween Minute-to-Win-It: Create one-minute challenges using cups, spoons, paper shapes or other safe household supplies.
  13. Costume Category Contest: Instead of choosing only one winner, create categories such as funniest, most creative, cutest and best DIY costume.
  14. Monster Drawing Challenge: Design a monster, then give it a name, favorite food, special power and funny fear.
  15. Halloween Story Starter: Begin with “At midnight, the pumpkin on the porch suddenly…” and let children finish the story.
  16. Halloween Hot Pumpkin: Pass a soft Halloween toy or decorated ball while music plays. When it stops, the person holding it completes a silly challenge.
  17. Halloween Photo Challenge: Make a list of fun photos to capture, such as a monster face, pumpkin, costume or Halloween decoration.
  18. Halloween Joke Challenge: Let everyone tell a Halloween joke and vote for the funniest one.
  19. Halloween Activity Jar: Fill a jar with quick prompts such as “walk like a zombie,” “draw a pumpkin” or “name five Halloween things.”
  20. Halloween Coloring Station: Set out printable coloring pages, crayons and markers for a calm activity between party games.
  21. No-Carve Pumpkin Design: Let children decorate pumpkin outlines or small pumpkins with stickers, markers and paper shapes instead of carving.
  22. Halloween Challenge Cards: Create cards with quick actions such as making a monster face, doing a witch laugh or naming five spooky things.
  23. Halloween Sorting Game: Sort paper pumpkins, bats or other shapes by color, size or category.
  24. Design Your Own Costume: Give kids a printable character or person outline and let them invent a costume on paper.
  25. Halloween Family Game Night: Combine bingo, charades, a scavenger hunt and a drawing challenge for a simple evening at home.

Halloween games for different ages

Ages 3–5

Keep activities visual and short. Picture bingo, coloring, freeze dance, simple scavenger hunts and pumpkin drawing work well.

Ages 6–8

Add word searches, memory games, charades, mystery boxes and creative drawing challenges.

Ages 9–12

Try team scavenger hunts, trivia, challenge cards, storytelling, photo challenges and more competitive party games.

Teens and mixed-age families

Use charades, costume categories, family trivia, photo challenges and team games that let different ages participate together.

Why screen-free Halloween activities work so well

A Halloween party does not need a screen to stay interesting. Hands-on activities give children something concrete to do while leaving room for imagination and social play.

Printable Halloween games are useful because the preparation happens before the party. You can print the bingo cards, activity prompts, coloring pages or scavenger hunt once and have them ready when the celebration begins.

The goal is simple: less time preparing during the party and more time enjoying it.

A simple Halloween party setup

You do not need 25 games at one party. Pick a small combination based on your space, guest count and children's ages.

  • Arrival: Coloring or a simple Halloween activity sheet.
  • Warm-up: Halloween I Spy or Bingo.
  • Main game: Scavenger Hunt or Charades.
  • Creative break: Pumpkin drawing or monster design.
  • Energy game: Freeze Dance.
  • Quiet finish: Coloring, memory game or story starter.

For a classroom or larger group, activity stations can make the setup easier. For a family night, two or three games may be all you need.
