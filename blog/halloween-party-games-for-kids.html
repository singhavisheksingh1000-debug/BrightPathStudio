25 Halloween party games for kids

  1. Halloween Bingo: Use pictures of pumpkins, ghosts, bats, candy, witches and friendly monsters.
  2. Halloween Scavenger Hunt: Give players a checklist of things to find, such as something orange, a pumpkin or a bat.
  3. Halloween I Spy: Challenge children to find Halloween colors, shapes and decorations.
  4. Pin the Spider on the Web: Make a paper web and let players place a paper spider near the center.
  5. Halloween Charades: Act out a ghost, witch, vampire, skeleton, zombie, bat or monster.
  6. Halloween Freeze Dance: Play Halloween music and freeze when it stops. Add silly monster poses.
  7. Halloween Would You Rather: Ask funny choices such as “ghost or vampire?”
  8. Pumpkin Drawing Challenge: Award categories such as funniest, cutest and most creative.
  9. Halloween Memory Game: Match pairs of Halloween pictures placed face down.
  10. Halloween Word Search: Use words such as pumpkin, ghost, bat, witch, candy and spider.
  11. Halloween Mystery Box: Guess safe, familiar objects hidden inside covered boxes.
  12. Halloween Minute Challenge: Create quick one-minute challenges using safe household supplies.
  13. Costume Category Contest: Give awards for funniest, cutest, most creative and best DIY costume.
  14. Monster Drawing Challenge: Design a monster and give it a name, power and funny fear.
  15. Halloween Story Starter: Start a spooky sentence and let every child add to the story.
  16. Hot Pumpkin: Pass a soft Halloween toy while music plays. When it stops, complete a silly challenge.
  17. Halloween Photo Challenge: Capture fun photos such as monster faces, pumpkins and costumes.
  18. Halloween Joke Challenge: Tell Halloween jokes and vote for the funniest.
  19. Halloween Activity Jar: Fill a jar with prompts such as “walk like a zombie.”
  20. Halloween Coloring Station: Set out printable coloring pages, crayons and markers.
  21. No-Carve Pumpkin Design: Decorate pumpkin outlines or small pumpkins with stickers and markers.
  22. Halloween Challenge Cards: Create quick actions such as making a monster face or witch laugh.
  23. Halloween Sorting Game: Sort paper pumpkins or bats by color, size or category.
  24. Design Your Own Costume: Give kids an outline and let them invent a costume on paper.
  25. Halloween Family Game Night: Combine bingo, charades, scavenger hunt and drawing for an easy evening.

A simple Halloween party setup

You do not need all 25 games. Choose a few based on your space, guest count and children's ages.

  • Arrival: Coloring or an activity sheet.
  • Warm-up: I Spy or Bingo.
  • Main game: Scavenger Hunt or Charades.
  • Creative break: Pumpkin drawing.
  • Energy game: Freeze Dance.
  • Quiet finish: Coloring, memory or storytelling.
