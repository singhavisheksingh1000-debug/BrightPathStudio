Ultimate Halloween Party Planning Checklist

Plan a memorable Halloween party with a simple checklist covering the details people often forget.

4–6 weeks before

Choose the date, guest list, theme, budget and venue. Send invitations and decide whether guests need costumes or special instructions.

2–3 weeks before

Plan decorations, games, music, food and drinks. Make a shopping list and prepare printable activities for different ages.

1 week before

Confirm RSVPs, buy nonperishables, prepare prizes, check decorations and create a timeline for setup and serving.

Party day

Set up activity stations, prepare food, keep essentials together and assign someone to capture photos. Leave a little breathing room between activities.

After the party

Pack reusable decorations, note what worked and save your favorite game ideas for next year.
