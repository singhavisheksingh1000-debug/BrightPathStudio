## Rules for every Pin

1. Match the Pin promise to the destination section exactly.
2. Use one primary CTA, not several competing actions.
3. Send product-intent Pins directly to the most relevant product section rather than the generic homepage.
4. Send free-resource Pins to the free-resource section first.
5. Keep the landing-page headline close to the Pin's search intent.
6. Do not use unrelated cross-sells as the first conversion action.
7. Use descriptive Pin filenames and alt text based on the actual topic.
8. Track each Pin campaign with UTM parameters when publishing links.

## Suggested UTM pattern

`?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=wedding&utm_content=wedding-budget-planner`

Change the campaign/content values for pregnancy, baby, ADHD, and individual Pin topics. Keep the destination path unchanged for the same intent cluster.
