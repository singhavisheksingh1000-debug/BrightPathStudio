What makes these resources useful?

Kids' printable activities can make it easier to offer a mix of creativity, movement, problem-solving, and quiet time. Choose an activity based on the child's age, interests, available supplies, and the amount of time you have.

  • Easy to start: many activities need only paper, pencils, crayons, or common household supplies.
  • Flexible: use them at home, in classrooms, at parties, or during homeschool time.
  • Screen-free: give children an offline option for creative play and learning.
