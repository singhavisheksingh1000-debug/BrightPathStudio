Download Help

After purchase

Use the download button or delivery link provided by the checkout service after your purchase. Save the files somewhere you can access them again.

If the download does not open

Try downloading the file again, using a modern browser, or opening the file on a desktop computer. For PDFs, use a current PDF reader.

Printing

Open the PDF in your preferred PDF reader and check the paper size and print scaling settings before printing.
